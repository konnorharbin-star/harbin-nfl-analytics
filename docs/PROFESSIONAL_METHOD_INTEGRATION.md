## Executable market audit (new)

Module: `scripts/professional_method_audit.py`; tests: `tests/test_professional_method_audit.py`; CI: `.github/workflows/professional-method-audit.yml`.

Input JSON keys: `entry_a`, `entry_b`, `selected`, optional `close_a`, `close_b`, `model`. Each quote requires: `event_id`, `kickoff_utc`, `market`, `period`, `participant` (use `GAME` for game markets), `line` (or `NA` for moneyline), `selection`, `american_odds`, `bookmaker`, `region`, `snapshot_id`, `quote_utc`, `captured_utc`, `source_type`, `source_url`. HOME/AWAY spread observations must **both** use `line_convention: HOME_SIGNED` and the home team's signed spread. Two prices *must originate from the same market snapshot* to calculate a two-sided no-vig probability; one price cannot be paired with an unrelated date or a different threshold.

Example run: `python -m scripts.professional_method_audit --input research/observed_quote_pair.json --output reports/market_method_audit.json` (example input file is not supplied; do not manufacture market observations).

An optional `model` object requires `source_kind: INDEPENDENT_MODEL`, `forecast_utc` before capture, `p_win`, `p_push`, `p_loss` with sum 1 and evidence metadata. Computes **diagnostic only** `p_win * decimal_profit - p_loss` and conditional break-even probability `(1-p_push)/decimal_odds`. No claim that the supplied independent probabilities are calibrated is made. CLV uses exact-contract selected-side entry decimal odds × no-vig conditional-on-no-push close probability − 1; this is a price-comparison diagnostic, not actual win probability and not proof of profit. Moneyline pushes/voids and settlement policies must be handled separately.

**Hard gate:** returns `decision: PASS`, `bet_authorized: false`, `execution_quote_verified: false`, `edge_validated: false` regardless of user-supplied model claims. No automatic wagering. This is an engineering audit, not a market discovery collector.

## Pre-registered future tests: not yet fit

1. **H1 Independent NFL score model:** Freeze all hyperparameters and as-of features. Compare per-game 2026 forward probabilistic log loss/Brier and error against fair contemporaneous-market benchmark, with team/week cluster bootstrap. Do not reuse inspected 2023–2025 as untouched validation.
2. **H2 QB/injury information timeliness:** Archive official starter/inactives, timestamped public market price, and freeze; assess whether a trained as-of adjustment improves market-anchored calibration. A mismatched prior-week QB proxy must PASS until fixed.
3. **H3 Granular NFL receiving props:** Build routes/dropback × target probability × catch probability × conditional yards as *distribution* with correlated game-script usage, not a product-of-means point forecast. Separate training and forward games; evaluate reliability and actual market-price EV.
4. **H4 Price dispersion and CLV:** Collect independent book quotes at same time for precisely the same market/threshold and regions. Test dispersion stability, stale/outlier behavior and prospective exact-contract CLV. Record missing/non-executable quotes as such. Avoid treating -3 vs +3.5 as complements.
5. **H5 Public analyst incremental information:** Compare source-complete frozen published picks to a market-only benchmark. Only include a published-analyst feature if repeated chronological out-of-sample improvement survives selection effects, missingness and multiple testing.
6. **H6 Risk and selection:** Only after reproducible account-price positive EV, estimate prediction error, push/void probabilities, staking feasibility and drawdown risk. Never claim a given Kelly fraction or EV floor itself validates profitability.

Only then can independent manual review even be considered; no auto bet.
