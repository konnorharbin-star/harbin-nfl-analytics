## Our independently executed evidence

[GitHub v4.7.0 full score/market diagnostic](https://github.com/konnorharbin-star/harbin-nfl-analytics/actions/runs/38183130119) reconstructed 208 NFL 2025 regular-season weeks 5–18 games with prior-week football-only data: independent **spread margin MAE 10.606922** vs archive-final market **9.992788**, independent **total MAE 10.483861** vs archive-final **10.081731**. Ridge-regularized football residual weight on 2023–24 development selected **ZERO** on both markets. These archive-final numbers do **not** have accepted pregame quote timestamps, so cannot be presented as executable forecast outcomes.

Our existing [QB challenger benchmark](../docs/candidate_benchmark.json) remains **SHADOW**: historical margin improvements in 2024 and 2025 against the *football* baseline, but still no independently documented market/profit benefit and no verified new starting-QB adjusted projection for every future event.

## Implemented evidence-based change: market FIRST as **reference**, independent score remains separate

Code: [scripts/market_first_reference.py](../scripts/market_first_reference.py). Tests: [tests/test_market_first_reference.py](../tests/test_market_first_reference.py). Cloud CI: [edge-lab-market-reference.yml](../.github/workflows/edge-lab-market-reference.yml), integrated with the existing professional price audit.

A fully formed **two-sided, same-book, same-region, same-event, exactly quoted-and-captured-at-the-same-instant** pregame spread and total observation can now produce a *market-based reference forecast*. For signed HOME spread handicap -3.5 (HOME favored), the reference expected HOME score margin is +3.5. Total of 45.5 implies home reference points (45.5+3.5)/2=24.5 and away (45.5-3.5)/2=21.0. These are implied market-centered **point references**, not independent score forecasts or calibrated discrete P(win/push/loss).

An independent football margin/total, already frozen with timestamp no later than the observation, can be displayed as a **disagreement diagnostic** (football margin minus market margin, football total minus market total). A disagreement **is not** +EV without calibrated probability and verified entry price. The code uses `professional_method_audit.fair_snapshot` to enforce complementary exact-contract two-sided quotes; rejects mismatched observation dates, kickoff, periods, books, region, spread sign convention, and any postgame or stale score timestamp; never substitutes an archived close or creates a fake line. Public/editorial quotes stay PROVISIONAL, not account-executable. Account-observed but unaccepted price remains unverified.

**What was deliberately NOT changed:** `nfl.ratings` parameters, `nfl.probability_runtime` activation rules, model betting EV releases, forward ledger predictions, account execution. v4.7.0 independent score predictions remain available separately. This v4.8.0 market-reference module is not a live odds collector and the tests use synthetic fixtures—not real offered BetMGM prices.

## Strict criteria for promoting any imported model as profit-seeking NFL signal

1. Public source, reproducible model version, documented input feature availability *before kickoff* and conservative independent chronological benchmark against market-only. For NFL starters do not use `home_qb_id`/actual starter in historical schedules absent an authentic pregame-as-of source.
2. A **separate pre-registered 2026 forward** replication with never-inspected games, model timestamps, exact source quotes, source times, regions and bookmaker identity. Market anchor measured at the SAME decision-time, not retrospectively closing prices.
3. Actual distribution P(win/push/loss), sportsbook-specific period/participant/threshold, proper scores and uncertainty; key-number pushes and voids priced correctly.
4. Conservative ROI/CLV after vig versus a matched actual quoted entry price, with uncertainty intervals and correct multiple-test selection treatment. Published analyst/model hypothetical odds are not account fills.
5. Independent verification/readback before core predictive or wagering gate changes. Never guarantee positive returns; paper research and PASS are default.

**Registry decision:** no externally researched model qualifies for **profitable NFL spread/total betting** promotion in v4.8.0. The *market reference* is adopted as an accuracy reporting and integrity tool only. This is supported by independent published methods and our own diagnostics, but is NOT a profitable edge.
