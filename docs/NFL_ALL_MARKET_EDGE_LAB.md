# Universal NFL market-edge research lab

## Purpose

Find the **best observable sportsbook mispricings**, rather than automatically choosing a winner or inflating the independent football model's probabilities. Examine BOTH opposing sides of every market with sufficient evidence and explicitly display every market family where our free data has no odds.

This is a broader search, **not** a guarantee that an edge exists in any particular game.

## Markets cataloged

- Full game: moneyline, spread, total, team total, alternate spread and alternate total.
- First and second halves: moneyline, spread, total and team total.
- Quarters: moneyline, spread, total, and first-quarter team totals.
- Individual players: pass yards/attempts/completions/TDs/interceptions, rush yards/attempts, receiving yards/receptions, anytime TD, long reception/rush and made field goals.

Coverage does **not** mean those markets are currently populated. The existing free ESPN/Action Network football feed supplies only game moneyline, spread and total. Player prop markets and alternate periods currently have `NO_FREE_SOURCE_OBSERVED`, not invented book offers or invented player-level probabilities.

## Source and identity requirements

Every price observation needs:
- Exact game ID and scheduled kickoff, period and market, selected side, a stable `subject_id` for player/team markets, and a normalized common point line.
- Named sportsbook, allowed free provider `espn` or `action_network`, and original source event identifier.
- True *source-offer update timestamp* and capture timestamp, not simply the time our downloader ran; both UTC aware and within 10 minutes of now, strictly before kickoff.
- An American price at least +100 or -100 by magnitude, and complementary opposing sides at identical market/line for a given sportsbook.

The universal scanner **does not** accept unaudited CSV claims of a fresh offer as proof. It is fed by our own provider adapters, which preserve the same fail-closed source-origin flags as the main price scanner. A future free player-prop adapter must identify exact player IDs, source terms and per-offer timestamps before providing data.

## Cross-book evaluation

For each instrument, compare the two-way implied prices from **at least three OTHER independent sportsbooks**. The target sportsbook is excluded from its own reference. Only contemporaneous observations (within five minutes) at the *same spread/total/prop threshold* count. Reference books must disagree by no more than 2.5 percentage points.

Calculate:
- Target book break-even probability: `1 / decimal_price`.
- Each other book's no-vig side probability: `raw_side_implied / (raw_side_implied + raw_opposing_implied)`.
- Research-only consensus median; conservative price-proxy EV using the **least favorable** comparator book.
- Keep only comparisons with a conservative proxy EV above 5%, as **RESEARCH_ONLY_RECHECK_PRICE**.

**This is not independently validated true EV.** Comparator books can move together, the market may be wrong, and a player prop may be subject to injuries, limits, settlement rules or stale prices. A cross-book consensus is not a calibrated sporting outcome model. Nothing here weakens the NFL's historical model/release gates or authorizes wagering.

## Free-data gaps to fix before real all-market coverage

1. Find and verify a legal no-cost source for player props, alternate lines, period/team totals, with *actual bookmaker-side quote-update timestamps and player/team identifiers*. Accept only sources and terms that allow this use. Without it, those markets stay explicitly missing.
2. Build independently calibrated player participation/usage/stat distributions from **pregame-available** nflverse rosters, PBP and weekly player data. Never use current injuries or game stats retroactively in a training sample. Compare against holdout no-vig two-sided prop markets, which also require point-in-time historical quotes.
3. Add a correlated score and possession model for team/half/quarter alternative distributions, with chronological holdout and market-only comparisons. Do not infer high-quality team-total or player prop probabilities from the unvalidated full-game Gaussian.
4. Log each original offer/book/side/line/time and ensuing book movement with exact market identity, for CLV evidence and after-game outcome grading. Preserve market coverage and exclusions.
5. Promote a specialist model only if its walk-forward test beats its own market baseline with uncertainty intervals and an untouched forward period.

## Outputs and operating rules

`docs/market_universe.json` is generated by every successful model run. It provides all market families cataloged, price coverage, exact exclusion counts, any conditional research watchlist and full limitations.

The market scan is independent of canonical core score estimation. Canonical probability, release and portfolio policies remain unchanged. **All recommendations are for human review; no automatic bets, sportsbook actions, deposits or paid services.** Do not advertise an edge in every game. Missing or unverified evidence means `NO VERIFIED EDGE`.

Free data context: the <Link> isn't required here; see nflverse's public player dataset releases and the existing ESPN/Action Network adapters, subject to data licensing and availability.
